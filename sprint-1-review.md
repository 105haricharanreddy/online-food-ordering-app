# Sprint 1 Review

## Sprint Goal

Allow customers to log in, browse restaurants and menus, and add food items to the cart.

## Features to Demonstrate

1. Customer Login
2. Restaurant Selection
3. Browse Menu
4. Food Selection
5. Add Food Items to Cart
6. View Cart

## Sprint Outcome

The team reviews the Customer Login, Browse Restaurants and Menu, and Shopping Cart user stories.

## Increment

The increment includes the features completed during Sprint 1. The team checks which features are completed and which still need work.

## Feedback

Record feedback from the lecturer, product owner, or other reviewers.

## Next Steps

Use the review feedback to improve the application and plan the next sprint.
